# Chess against AI models

A platform where people play chess against AI models that choose their own moves.

## Language

**Human player**:
A person playing chess against a model opponent.

**Model opponent**:
An AI model that plays one side of a chess game and chooses its own moves.

**Connected proxy**:
A human player's existing CLIProxyAPI setup that provides access to their model subscriptions.

**Model catalog**:
The model entries advertised by a human player's connected proxy.

**Model family**:
A named group of related AI models, such as GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, or Kimi.

**Model switch**:
A change of model opponent within an existing chess match.

**Match conversation**:
The accumulated instructions, game inputs, and model replies in a chess match, including any plans or reasoning the models returned.

**Move attempt**:
A move proposed by a model opponent that may be legal or illegal.
