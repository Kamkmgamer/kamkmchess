# AI Chess MVP implementation plan

Created 2026-10-16 from the owner-approved specification. [Open the Linear project](https://linear.app/kamkm/project/ai-chess-mvp-8a102853ce2c).

The completed [decision map](https://linear.app/kamkm/issue/KHA-5/find-the-way-to-the-ai-chess-mvp) and its 20 decision/research children remain Done in the Specification agreed milestone. Implementation work is tracked as separate top-level project tickets, with native blockers. All 27 new tickets are unassigned in Backlog.

The approved sources are `docs/spec/mvp-handoff.md` and `docs/spec/mvp-acceptance.json`, reviewed commit `35444e0`, approval metadata `e9f0c9f`, with final agreement recorded on [Agree on MVP acceptance criteria and the specification handoff](https://linear.app/kamkm/issue/KHA-14/agree-on-mvp-acceptance-criteria-and-the-specification-handoff). Linear resolutions remain canonical.

## Scope and evidence

This backlog covers implementation and verification; creating it performed neither. Every ticket includes an outcome, scope, completion evidence and acceptance IDs. Across the tickets all 79 approved acceptance cases are mapped. `docs/spec/implementation-tickets.json` records ticket-to-case ownership and the 53 native dependency edges. Cases may appear on a module ticket and again on integrated/native verification tickets. No product acceptance status changed.

Preserve independently chosen model moves, browser-owned rules/clocks/direct requests, exact checked adjudication or honest saved pending, continuous clocks, complete retained attributed history, Undo archives, device-local AI credentials, preserved offline conflicts and complete screen-reader play in the approved Classic workspace.

Managed free tiers are the starting point; bring paid upgrades to the owner before charges. Show costs without a fixed target or price-based model restriction. About 40 model turns is a reporting workload, with diagnostics/retries/cache/failures included where billed. Native devices, private credentials, live/spend authorization, no-cost backup feasibility and the final public-launch decision remain explicit execution prerequisites on their tickets.
