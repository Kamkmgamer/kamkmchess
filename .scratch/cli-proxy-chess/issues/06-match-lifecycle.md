# Define the MVP match lifecycle

Publication: Draft for Linear
Label: wayfinder:grilling
Mode: HITL
Blocked by: None

## Question

What are the complete rules and player actions for a single MVP match, from starting it to ending or resuming it?

Decide color selection, turn order, supported chess rules, promotion, checkmate and draws, resignation, clocks or the absence of clocks, undo or restart, rematch, and behavior on refresh, navigation, and interruption. Set the minimum persistence expectation and whether identities or accounts are required. Use concrete player scenarios and keep the scope focused on playing a match against a model.
