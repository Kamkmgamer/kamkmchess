# Choose the model catalog and opponent selection rules

Publication: Draft for Linear
Label: wayfinder:grilling
Mode: HITL
Blocked by: [Document CLIProxyAPI model discovery and move requests](02-proxy-api.md)

## Question

How will players choose an opponent from the models exposed by their connected CLIProxyAPI?

Decide whether the MVP offers every discovered model, a tested allowlist, manual model IDs, or another clear policy. Define names and aliases, capability eligibility, unavailable models, discovery failures, changes during a game, and the evidence needed to call a model supported. Agree on the first live verification candidates from the developer's proxy without assuming a fixed set of model families or making untested strength claims.
