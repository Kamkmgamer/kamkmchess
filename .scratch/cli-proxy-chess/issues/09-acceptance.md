# Agree on MVP acceptance criteria and the specification handoff

Publication: Draft for Linear
Label: wayfinder:grilling
Mode: HITL
Blocked by: [Validate the connection and chess match interface](07-interface.md), [Choose the application architecture and data boundaries](08-architecture.md)

## Question

What concrete evidence will show that the planned MVP meets the agreed product promise, and is any decision still missing before implementation?

Define the minimum acceptance scenarios for connecting a supported player-owned proxy, selecting a supported model, finishing a legal match, handling model and connection failures, protecting the chosen credential boundary, and using the agreed devices and inputs. Reconcile the previous ticket resolutions into a specification outline and trace its requirements to their canonical tickets. Surface remaining decisions as tickets rather than silently choosing them. Resolve this ticket only with the human's agreement that the specification is ready to hand off.
